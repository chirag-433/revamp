# One-minute SIH demo cut

Use a prepared synthetic institution with two open cases in different Bureaus.

| Time | Screen and narration |
|---|---|
| 0–10 sec | Open the case register. “A single institution may have work in more than one Bureau. Separate registers make that hard to see.” |
| 10–22 sec | Search the institution by Permanent ID and open its profile. Show the two Bureau case records and their source history. |
| 22–36 sec | Open the overlap or conflict review. Point to the matched institution, Bureau names, case statuses and explanation. |
| 36–48 sec | Record a review decision or accept a pending transfer as the receiving Bureau. Show the new action in the case history. |
| 48–60 sec | Open Reports. “These are synthetic benchmark results. Real source data, mentor-approved rules and a production pilot are still pending.” |

If the review screen is slow, show the prepared case detail and explain the decision path without claiming that a transfer was accepted.
